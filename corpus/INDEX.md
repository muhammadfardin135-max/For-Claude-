## Honest summary

This corpus is **good at cold email and nothing else yet**. Within cold email
it is genuinely strong: three independent sources, roughly 260,000 emails of
data behind the mechanics, and one source supplying real copy.

Outside cold email it thins out fast. There is no closing material, no pitch
structure, and — despite Josh Braun's central claim being about objections —
no recovered objection responses.

See `playbook/principles.md` → "Where the corpus is currently silent".
