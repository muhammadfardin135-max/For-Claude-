# Jason Bay / Outbound Squad

## 1. Core thesis
Recovered only at the level of a positioning statement: outbound should make
the seller "too good to ignore" by disqualifying hard, giving value before
asking for time, and taking a strong point of view on the buyer's problem
rather than presenting features. The underlying claim is that most outbound
fails because it asks for time it hasn't earned.

## 2. Operating frameworks

### Too Good To Ignore
- **Use when:** Overall outbound posture.
- **Steps (as stated on the page — no elaboration given):**
  1. **Disqualify** — "Ruthlessly disqualify opportunities unworthy of our
     energy, time, and attention."
  2. **Offer** — "Be consultative and make strategic 'gives' to buyers early
     and often to earn their time."
  3. **Provoke** — "Engage buyers with a strong point of view on their problem
     that compels action now."
- **Why it works (per source):** Not stated.
- **Failure mode:** Not stated.
