# Gong — cold email data (28M+ emails)

## 1. Core thesis
Cold email still works, but the performance gap between top reps and average
reps is enormous and comes from specific, measurable behaviours — not volume or
talent. The single largest lever is **not pitching**: pitching in a cold email
cuts reply rates by as much as 57%. Emails should be built from the buyer's
priorities and language, not the seller's product.

## 2. Operating frameworks

### Priority-based messaging
- **Use when:** Writing any cold email body.
- **Steps:**
  1. Identify the buyer's actual priority (industry problem, trend, leadership
     statement), not your product's capability.
  2. Write in the buyer's own terminology.
  3. Reference specific logos or industries similar to the prospect's.
  4. Do not pitch the platform.
- **Why it works (per source):** Focus on problems and customer language rather
  than solutions; pitching triggers the 57% reply-rate collapse.
- **Failure mode:** Reaching for buzzwords to sound credible — costs up to
  17.9% of open rate.

### Priority-based subject lines
- **Use when:** Every cold email.
- **Steps:** Build the subject from one of — an industry problem, an industry
  trend, a leadership quote, a shared connection, or a one-word pattern
  interrupt.
- **Failure mode:** Marketing language, numbers, questions, AI mentions, and
  social proof all underperform here.

## 5. Non-negotiables and prohibitions
- **DO NOT** pitch in a cold email — reduces reply rates by as much as **57%**.
- **DO NOT** use industry buzzwords — "TCO and MTTR sound really cool, but
  you're not fooling anyone." Buzzwords and numbers cut open rates by up to
  **17.9%**.
- **DO NOT** talk about AI — competitors have saturated the topic.
- **DO NOT** use "all-in-one" or "single pane of glass."
- **DO NOT** promise ROI multiples like "10x return."
- **DO NOT** put numbers or questions in the subject line.
- **DO NOT** use marketing language or mention AI in the subject line.
- **DO NOT** lead with social proof in the subject line — it underperforms
  there (note: this is subject-line specific; social proof in the *body* is
  recommended as specific logos).

## 7. Metrics & benchmarks
- **344** cold emails sent per meeting booked, for the average rep.
- Top reps book **8.1x** more meetings than average.
- Top 25% land **4.3x** more meetings than average.
- Top performers get **4.2x** more replies than average.
- Top performers get **2.1x** higher open rates than average.
- **10%+ reply rate** = "gold standard" for top reps.
- Optimal length: **100 words or fewer**; **3-4 sentences** generate the
  highest reply rates.
- Pitching costs up to **57%** of reply rate.
- Buzzwords and numbers cost up to **17.9%** of open rate.

## 8. Context limits
B2B tech/SaaS sales orgs with SDR functions, drawn from Gong's customer base
(revenue-intelligence users). The 344-emails-per-meeting figure reflects that
population and should not be quoted at a business with a different motion,
price point, or list quality. Cold email first touch only — silent on discovery,
pitch, objections, and closing.
