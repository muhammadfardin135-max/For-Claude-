# $100M Leads — Alex Hormozi

## 1. Core thesis
Every business problem reduces to a lack of leads, and growth is a function of
attention → conversations → sales. There are only four ways to advertise, and
most operators run one of them at partial capacity while believing the market
is exhausted. Success comes from disciplined volume, not creativity.

## 2. Operating frameworks

### The Core Four
- **Use when:** Deciding where leads come from at all.
- **The four channels:**
  1. **Warm outreach** — private contact with people who already know you.
  2. **Free content** — public posts that build a warm audience over time.
  3. **Cold outreach** — private contact with strangers.
  4. **Paid ads** — public reach bought from a platform.
- **The split:** warm/cold outreach = private, one-to-one. Content/ads =
  public, one-to-many.
- **Why it works (per source):** The claim is that these are the only four
  channels any business has ever used; the opportunity is in running the ones
  you have neglected, not in finding a fifth.
- **Failure mode:** Believing a channel is exhausted while running it at
  partial capacity.

### The Rule of 100
- **Use when:** Setting daily activity volume.
- **Steps:** Do 100 primary marketing actions every day for 100 consecutive
  days — 100 outreaches, or 100 minutes of content, or $100 of ad spend.
- **Why it works (per source):** Volume and consistency over creativity.

### Lead magnet design
- **Definition (quoted):** "a product or service, like an ebook or
  consultation, that solves a small but significant problem for your potential
  customers."
- **Steps:**
  1. Start with a precisely defined problem the audience has.
  2. Build a solution that ALSO reveals their need for the core offer.
  3. Choose one of two shapes: give one key step of a multi-step solution, OR
     reveal symptoms that point to a larger problem.

### Cold outreach (as summarized — thin)
1. Use list brokers for segmented lists; research social media manually.
2. Personalize based on that research.
3. Follow up **2–3 times** before abandoning a lead.

### Content: grab / keep / satisfy interest
Effective content must grab interest, keep interest, and satisfy interest —
through topic choice, storytelling, and delivered value.

## 8. Context limits
Written for founders and small-business operators across many industries
(gyms, agencies, info products, local services), not for enterprise B2B sales
teams. The Rule of 100 assumes an operator who controls their own time. Nothing
here addresses a structured B2B sales cycle, discovery calls, or multi-
stakeholder deals — it is a demand-generation book, not a selling-conversation
book. It sits at a different layer from the cold-email sources in this corpus:
Hormozi decides WHICH CHANNEL, they decide WHAT THE MESSAGE SAYS.
